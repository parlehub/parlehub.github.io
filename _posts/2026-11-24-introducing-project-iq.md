*Every project produces a trail of meetings, decisions, tasks and documents. Project IQ is how Parle keeps that trail connected, permission-safe and ready to answer questions.*

**November 24, 2026:** Parle today announced **Project IQ**, a connected record of the work on a project: who was involved, which meetings happened, what was decided, what is still to do, and where each piece came from. It sits inside the projects your team already runs in Parle, and it is available now for Business Elite and Enterprise organizations.

## Why this matters

Ask a services firm where the knowledge about a client engagement lives and the honest answer is everywhere. The scope is in a statement of work in one library. The decisions are in the memories of the people who attended a call in March. The action items are in someone's notes, a chat thread, and a task tool that is two weeks out of date. When a new person joins, or a partner returns from leave, someone spends a day reconstructing the story from fragments.

That reconstruction is a real cost. It shows up as rework, as disputes about what was agreed, and as the senior person who is asked the same question for the fifth time. An AI assistant does not fix it by itself. An assistant that cannot see how the pieces connect can only search them, and a search for "what did we decide" returns ninety minutes of transcript.

## What Project IQ is

Project IQ is a small, typed set of connections built outward from your projects, clients and departments. The things in it are people, clients, projects, meetings, decisions, action items, tasks, deliverables and documents. The links between them are labeled, so Parle can tell a decision that was made in a meeting from a task that was raised in a chat.

Two design choices matter more than the rest. The graph stores **pointers and metadata, not copies of your content**; when something needs to be quoted, Parle reads it live, with the access of the person asking. And nothing in it is a score about a person. People appear as names on records, never as numbers.

## Why we built it

Parle began as a shared workspace for a team's AI conversations, organized by project and governed by budgets and roles. A project, in a services firm, is more than a folder of conversations. It is a client engagement with a scope, a calendar of meetings, a list of commitments and a cast of people. The conversations are only one part of the record.

Project IQ connects the rest of that record to the conversations, so the assistant your team already uses can answer from the whole project, and so a person can open the project and see the same picture the assistant sees.

## Availability

Project IQ is available for Business Elite and Enterprise organizations. An administrator can switch it off, or switch off any single source of data, at any time, and the controls for what Parle may keep are covered in [Your data, your control](/blog/2027/01/05/your-data-your-control/). Some capabilities, such as suggesting decisions from a transcript, are off until an administrator turns them on.
