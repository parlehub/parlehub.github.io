How we write these

Every claim about another product is taken from that vendor's own documentation, dated, and linked. Each page carries a section on where the other product is the better buy, because there are real cases where it is, and the most obvious one applies to all three: they hand you one vendor and one invoice, with nothing to set up. ParleHub runs on keys you bring, which means a provider account and someone who owns it.

One thing we are careful not to claim, because the marketing in this category invites it: none of these products give you model usage for free. Anthropic prices Enterprise per seat plus the tokens you consume. Google's seats carry a quota and charge beyond it. OpenAI covers ordinary chat in the seat and meters the heavier work against credits. Everyone is running a meter. The question worth asking is whose meter it is and whose rate it runs at, and on ParleHub the answer is yours.

These products move quickly. If you find something here that has gone stale, tell us and we will correct it.
