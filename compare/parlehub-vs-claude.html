Read this first

ParleHub runs on Claude. Anthropic's models are one of the four provider families you can connect, alongside OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Azure OpenAI. This page is not an argument that Claude is the wrong model. It compares Anthropic's own team workspace with ours, for a firm deciding where its people should be having those conversations and how the cost gets booked.

The short version

Three differences that change the answer

01

The unit of the budget

Claude caps spend on the organization, a group, or a named user. ParleHub caps it on the project, which is the engagement, matter, or account you already bill against. Personal and org-wide caps sit underneath as backstops.

02

Whose code the cost carries

Every ParleHub project carries your own expense code, and that code comes through in the CSV export. The spend arrives in finance already labelled with the client reference your general ledger recognises, with no join to build.

03

What "shared" means

A shared Claude Project shares its knowledge and instructions; chats inside it stay private to their author unless someone deliberately shares them. In ParleHub the thread itself is the shared object, live, with presence.

Cost allocation

Per-user caps answer a question your firm is not asking

A per-user cap tells you that a senior consultant spent $340 last month. Your partners want to know what the Ashworth deal cost. One person works four engagements in a month and the other three people on Ashworth each have their own cap, so the number has to be reassembled by hand, every month, from a report that was never organised that way.

  • check_circle Anyone blocked can request an increase in one click, routed to an admin to approve or reject

Collaboration

Shared context is not the same as a shared conversation

A Claude Project shares the knowledge base and the instructions, so nobody re-uploads the same eleven exhibits. Anthropic's documentation is explicit that the chats inside it are not shared by default and stay inaccessible to other members unless the author shares them. The reasoning that produced last Thursday's answer is still in one person's history.

  • check_circle Every thread in a ParleHub project is visible to its members as it happens
  • check_circle Live presence shows who is viewing, typing, or waiting on a response, and the streamed reply appears to everyone watching
  • check_circle Keyword and semantic search across every conversation in the project, so a new joiner reads the work instead of rebuilding it
  • check_circle A useful personal chat can be promoted into the shared project
Model access

One vendor's models, or the models you already pay for

Claude Team and Enterprise are a way to buy Claude. Enterprise is priced per seat plus the tokens your org consumes, so the meter is running either way; what you are buying is Anthropic's meter rather than your own. It also means the workspace and the model are the same commercial decision, and changing your mind about one changes the other. With us the two are separate purchases: bring the provider contract you already signed, or use models we host and pay for what you use.

There is a third case neither vendor covers. A good deal of the cheap, capable capacity now comes from labs your firm will never contract with, because a forty-person practice is not a procurement target for anyone and self-hosting an open-weight model is an infrastructure project nobody asked for. We host those models, so routine drafting and summarising can run on something that costs a fraction of a frontier call while the analysis a partner signs stays on the model you trust for it. Spend is tracked per project either way, so whether the cheaper model was good enough stops being an argument and becomes a number on the engagement.

  • check_circle Or skip the keys entirely and use models we host, charged by what you use. Run both at once if you want, on the same ledger
  • check_circle That includes open-weight models from outside the big three, which your firm will never sign a contract with and should not have to
  • check_circle Calls run under your firm's own provider agreement, which is the agreement your client questionnaire asks about
  • check_circle Switch model per conversation, deliberately, with no automatic routing or silent downgrade
  • check_circle Every call lands in one ledger, so the comparison across providers is like for like
Portability

The best model for your work in 2027 is not the one you signed up for

Model prices fall in steps, and each step is an opportunity you can only take if taking it is cheap. When the workspace belongs to the model vendor, switching means rebuilding it: the projects, the instructions, the uploaded knowledge, the history your team searches. Most firms price that at more than the saving and stay where they are, which is the point of selling the workspace and the model together.

ParleHub keeps the provider at the edge. Everything your team builds sits above it and does not move when the model does.

  • check_circle Point one conversation at a different vendor's model when a single question needs it, keeping the thread, the project files, and the agent tooling
  • check_circle Move a project or the whole organization onto another provider as a settings change; projects, threads, files, budgets, project codes, roles, and the audit trail are untouched
  • check_circle Your keys or ours, so moving spend to a cheaper model is a commercial decision rather than a migration project. The same budgets, caps, project codes and ledger apply either way
  • check_circle One ledger measures every provider, so the comparison that justifies the move comes from your own usage rather than a vendor's pricing page

Where Claude is the better buy

Three reasons to pick Anthropic instead

A comparison page that finds nothing good to say about the other product is a sales sheet. These are the cases where we would tell you to buy Claude.

  • arrow_right_alt You want Anthropic's whole model line, first-party. Every Claude model Anthropic ships, on the day it ships, metered by the company that built it. We can run Anthropic models on your own key, and we host a curated set of models ourselves, but a curated set is a choice someone made and the first-party line is not.
  • arrow_right_alt Your cost centres are departments, not clients. If AI spend belongs to Marketing and Legal rather than to engagements, group and per-user caps mapped from your directory are a closer fit than project budgets, and Anthropic has them.
  • arrow_right_alt Your main use is engineering work. ParleHub is not a coding tool and has no coding harness. Anthropic's developer surfaces are built for that and we are not competing for it.
