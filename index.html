Cost allocation

Timesheets for your AI agents

Your people book their hours to a client. Your AI books nothing. It arrives as one provider invoice with no client on it and lands in overhead, where the practices that barely touch AI quietly subsidise the ones that live on it.

  • check_circle A budget scoped to the project, not the person, because your people work across six of them
  • check_circle Your own project code on every project, carried through to the export
  • check_circle Spend and token usage broken down by project, person, provider, or model, over any range
  • check_circle One canonical token ledger behind both the report and the enforcement, so there is a single number

Every project carries your own expense code, straight through to the export. AI cost lands where every other cost already lands, so you can recharge it, price it into a fixed fee, or cut the engagement type that does not pay.

Control

Find out before the invoice, not after

Metered spend with no ceiling is the opposite of how a firm plans. One enthusiastic user, one runaway loop, and the number turns up at month end with an explanation attached to it.

  • check_circle Pre-flight enforcement blocks the send before it exceeds the project budget
  • check_circle An 80% warning state, so the team sees it coming
  • check_circle Personal and org-wide caps stacked as backstops above every project
  • check_circle Blocked? Request an increase in one click, routed straight to an admin

The ceiling becomes a number you set rather than a number you discover, and it does not stop the work, because there is a path through it.

Shared context

The best work your firm did last week is in a closed tab

Private chat histories mean context rebuilt from scratch at every client switch, by every person. Two people on the same engagement ask the same question a day apart, against different documents, and get different answers. When someone rolls off, what they worked out leaves with them.

  • check_circle Shared project threads every member can see, live
  • check_circle Presence indicators for who is viewing, typing, or waiting on a reply
  • check_circle Keyword and semantic search across every conversation in the project
  • check_circle Promote a personal chat into a shared project when it turns out to matter
  • check_circle Branch by editing and resending, without losing the original

  • check_circle Real tool calls against the project's own files, so the work is grounded in your documents
  • check_circle Exact find-and-replace and diffs, so a reviewer sees what changed, not a silent rewrite
